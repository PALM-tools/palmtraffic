PIDS_TRAFFIC

Description of the structure of the PALM Traffic driver version 1.0.

Dimensions:
time         : Number of timesteps.
s            : Number of the grid cells with transportation.
car_type     : Number of car types.
field_length : Length of the text fields timestamp, car_type.

Variables:
timestamp(time, field_length)    : Timestamp (format the same as PALM VSR emission).
car_type(car_type, field_length) : Car type description.
car_cd(car_type)                 : Car drag coefficient (unitless).
car_length(car_type)             : Car length (m).
car_width(car_type)              : Car width (m).
car_height(car_type)             : Car height (m).
i(s)                             : i coordinate of the grid cell.
j(s)                             : j coordinate of the grid cell.
k(s)                             : k coordinate of the grid cell.
width(s)                         : Width of the street (m).
slope(s)                         : Slope of the street in car direction (%).
dirx(s)                          : Car movement direction (x unit coordinate).
diry(s)                          : Car movement direction (y unit coordinate).
frac(s)                          : Fraction of the grid cell in the car stream (unitless).
intensity(time, s, car_type)     : Transportation intensity (cars per hour).
heat(time, s, car_type)          : Heat produced by car (W per car).
speed(time, s, car_type)         : Speed of the car (m/s).
